A Journey Towards Holistic Wellness

Ayuryoga International is a wellness destination in Mauritius offering Ayurvedic therapies, wellness treatments, beauty care and physiotherapy.

Our approach is inspired by the traditional principles of Ayurveda and complemented by professional wellness and physiotherapy services.

We believe wellness is not simply about a single treatment. It is about taking time to care for yourself, understanding your individual needs and creating habits that support a healthier and more balanced lifestyle.
