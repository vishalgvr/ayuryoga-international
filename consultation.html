Ayurvedic Consultation

Discover Your Unique Constitution

An Ayurvedic consultation is the first step towards understanding your body's unique needs. According to Ayurveda, every individual is born with a unique combination of the three doshas: Vata, Pitta, and Kapha.

Our expert practitioners will conduct a comprehensive assessment of your physical, mental, and emotional health to determine your Prakriti (natural constitution) and Vikriti (current imbalances).

Based on this assessment, we provide personalized recommendations on diet, lifestyle, and appropriate therapies to help you achieve optimal balance.
