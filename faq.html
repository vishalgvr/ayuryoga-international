Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our therapies, bookings, and services.

What services does Ayuryoga International offer?
Ayuryoga International offers Ayurveda treatments, wellness therapies, beauty care and physiotherapy services.
Where is Ayuryoga International located?
Ayuryoga International currently has locations in Moka and Grand Baie, Mauritius.
Do I need an appointment?
Appointments are recommended. Contact our team to discuss availability and your preferred treatment.
Can I choose between Moka and Grand Baie?
Yes. You can select your preferred location when making an enquiry.
Which Ayurveda treatment is suitable for me?
The most appropriate treatment depends on your individual requirements. Discuss your needs with our Ayurvedic team before selecting a specialized treatment.
Do you offer massage treatments?
Yes. The wellness menu includes Ayurvedic, Swedish, deep tissue, aroma, stone and reflexology massage options, among others.
Do you provide physiotherapy?
Yes. Ayuryoga offers physiotherapy services covering areas such as neck pain, sciatica, frozen shoulder, arthritis, rehabilitation and mobility-related conditions.
How long do treatments take?
Treatment durations vary. Ayurveda treatments currently range from approximately 15 to 90 minutes, while wellness and beauty services have different durations.
How do I contact Ayuryoga?
You can contact the Moka branch at 58074009 or the Grand Baie branch at 59429564.
