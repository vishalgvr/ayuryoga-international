The Healing Power of Padabhyanga

In classical Ayurveda, foot care (*Padabhyanga*) is revered as a gateway to overall wellness. The soles contain vital Marma points directly connected to your nervous and sensory systems.

Marma & Nerve Vitality

Gentle pressure on key Marma points activates nerve channels, relieving stress throughout the body.

Deep Moisture & Repair

Botanical scrubs and nourishing herbal oils lock in moisture to repair dry, cracked heels.

Stimulates microcirculation to reduce fatigue, fluid retention, and heaviness in tired feet.
