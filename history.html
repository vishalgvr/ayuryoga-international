Our History & Sacred Roots

Carrying forward 5,000 years of unbroken Vedic wisdom from the heartlands of Kerala to the serene tropical shores of Mauritius.

The Genesis

A Timeless Vision of Authentic Healing

Ayuryoga International was founded with a profound mission: to preserve and practice classical Ayurveda in its purest, most authentic form. Long before wellness became a modern lifestyle trend, Ayurveda flourished as the sacred science of life—a comprehensive medical and philosophical system originating in ancient India.

Recognizing the growing need for genuine holistic health amidst fast-paced modern living, our founders set out to create a sanctuary where age-old healing protocols, medicated herbal oils, and certified Vaidya consultations could be experienced with uncompromised integrity.

"Ayurveda does not merely treat illness; it awakens the body's innate intelligence to restore balance, vitality, and inner peace."

Classical Foundations

Rooted in Ancient Texts

Our therapies and therapeutic oils are prepared and administered in strict accordance with the classical treatises of Ayurveda.

Ashtanga Hridaya

The quintessential classical compilation that guides our herbal formulations, rejuvenation therapies, and seasonal detoxification rituals.

Tridosha Harmony

Every individual is unique. We evaluate your personal constitution (Vata, Pitta, Kapha) to tailor treatments that bring your bodily energies into balance.

Key Milestones

Our Journey Through the Years

From our founding vision to becoming Mauritius' most trusted Ayurvedic wellness establishment.

2012 · Foundation

The Sacred Inspiration

Deep studies and collaborations with Kerala's traditional Vaidya families to establish an authentic standard of classical Ayurvedic medicine outside India.

2015 · Flagship Center

Opening the Moka Sanctuary

Ayuryoga International opened its primary healing center in Moka, Mauritius, offering authentic Panchakarma, Shirodhara, and doctor consultations.

2018 · Island-wide Expansion

Grand Baie & Curepipe Branches

Expanded our footprint with dedicated centers in the north (Grand Baie) and central highlands (Curepipe) to make traditional care accessible across Mauritius.

2021 · Integrated Care

Holistic Wellness & Physiotherapy

Integrated professional physiotherapy, Western wellness therapies, and natural Ayurvedic beauty care into our treatment catalog for comprehensive client health.

Present · The Legacy Continues

A Benchmark for Healing Excellence

Over a decade of transforming thousands of lives, maintaining the highest standards of authentic practice, certified practitioners, and tranquil healing spaces.
