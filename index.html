Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick answers about our authentic Ayurvedic therapies, doctor consultations, clinic locations, and booking process.

What authentic therapies does Ayuryoga International offer?
+
We offer a comprehensive range of classical Kerala Ayurveda therapies (such as Abhyangam, Shirodhara, Kizhi herbal poultices, and Vasthi), restorative wellness massages, natural organic beauty care, botanical facials, rejuvenating body scrubs, Ayurvedic hair care, and reflexology hand & foot treatments.
Where is your Ayuryoga Moka clinic located?
+
Our flagship Moka clinic is centrally located at Leclezio Avenue, right beside Moka Business Centre. We also welcome guests at our Grand Baie clinic (Lorient Lane, Chemin Vingt Pieds) and Curepipe clinic.
Do I need to book an appointment in advance?
+
Yes, advance bookings are strongly recommended to guarantee therapist availability and allow adequate time for personalized preparation. You can easily book online or reach out via WhatsApp at +230 58074009.
How do I know which Ayurvedic therapy is right for my body?
+
We offer specialized Ayurvedic Doctor Consultations featuring traditional Nadi Pariksha (Pulse Diagnosis). Our experienced doctors assess your unique Dosha balance (Vata, Pitta, Kapha) and curate an individualized treatment plan tailored to your health goals.
How long do individual treatment sessions take?
+
Treatment durations generally range between 45 to 90 minutes depending on the therapy selected. Comprehensive wellness and rejuvenation packages can extend from 90 to 180 minutes.
Are the herbal oils and ingredients 100% natural?
+
Yes. All our herbal oils, medicated decoctions, scrubs, and face masks are authentic, pure, and prepared according to classical Ayurvedic formulations without artificial additives or harsh chemicals.
