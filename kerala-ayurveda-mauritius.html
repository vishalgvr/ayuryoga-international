What Is Kerala Ayurveda?

Kerala Ayurveda is a traditional form of Ayurvedic practice originating from Kerala, India. The region is widely recognised for its rich Ayurvedic heritage, traditional therapies, herbal preparations and wellness practices.

Ayurveda focuses on maintaining harmony between the body, mind and lifestyle. Traditional Ayurvedic treatments use herbal oils, natural ingredients, therapeutic massage techniques and specialised procedures based on individual requirements.

Unlike an ordinary spa massage, Ayurvedic therapies are rooted in a traditional wellness philosophy that considers individual needs and overall well-being.

Popular Kerala Ayurvedic Treatments at Ayuryoga International

Discover some of the traditional treatments available at Ayuryoga International:

Abhyangam – Traditional Full Body Ayurvedic Massage

Abhyangam is a traditional Ayurvedic massage performed using herbal oils and specific massage techniques. It is valued for its relaxing experience and is traditionally incorporated into Ayurvedic wellness routines.

Ksheeradhara – Traditional Herbal Liquid Therapy

Ksheeradhara is a specialised Ayurvedic therapy involving the gentle, continuous pouring of warm medicated milk or other prescribed liquids over the body or a specific area, depending on the treatment protocol. It is a traditional wellness practice associated with relaxation.

Shirodhara – The Art of Relaxation

Shirodhara is a well-known Ayurvedic treatment in which a continuous stream of warm liquid, commonly herbal oil, is gently poured over the forehead. This traditional therapy is appreciated for its calming and relaxing experience.

Herbal Hot Pouch Therapy (Kizhi)

Herbal hot pouch therapy, traditionally known as Kizhi, uses heated herbal preparations wrapped in cloth pouches. The pouches are gently applied to selected areas of the body as part of a traditional Ayurvedic treatment to promote comfort and ease muscle tension.

Ayurvedic Facial Treatments

Ayurvedic facials use traditional herbal ingredients and natural skincare preparations to provide a refreshing facial experience. These treatments are suitable for individuals interested in exploring herbal skincare and traditional beauty practices.
