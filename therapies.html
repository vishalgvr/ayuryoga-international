Pillar 01 — Ancient Wisdom

Classical Ayurvedic Treatments

Ayurveda is one of the world's most sophisticated traditional healing sciences, originating in India over 5,000 years ago. At Ayuryoga International, our authentic therapies are administered with specially formulated warm herbal oils, medicated decoctions, and traditional techniques.

Whether you are seeking relief from muscular tension, joint stiffness, mental stress, or simply looking to detoxify and revitalize your vital energies (Doshas), our classical Ayurvedic therapies provide holistic, time-tested restorative care.

Abhyangam 45 / 60 Mins

Traditional rhythmic full-body massage using warm medicated herbal oils to improve blood flow, soothe the nervous system, nourish tissues, and enhance cellular vitality.

Sirodhara 60 Mins

A continuous, gentle stream of warm herbal oil or medicated milk poured over the forehead (Ajna chakra) to melt mental fatigue, relieve insomnia, and induce profound mental peace.

Kizhi Poultice Therapies 45 / 60 Mins

Warm herbal boluses (Podikizhi, Naranga Kizhi, Navara Kizhi) applied over the body to alleviate joint stiffness, ease muscular inflammation, and enhance mobility.

Vasthi & Kati Vasthi 45 Mins

Therapeutic herbal dough reservoirs retaining warm medicinal oils over specific areas such as the lower back, neck, or knees to alleviate localized tension and disc strain.

Nasyam 30 Mins

Purifying nasal administration of herb-infused oils following facial massage and mild steam, cleansing sinuses, sharpening sensory organs, and soothing tension headaches.

Udwarthanam 60 Mins

Invigorating therapeutic dry herbal powder scrub massaged in upward strokes to stimulate lymphatic drainage, enhance skin texture, and support healthy metabolism.

Restorative Wellness & Massages

From signature full-body relaxation sessions to focused deep tissue bodywork, warm basalt stone therapies, and herbal steam baths, each session is tailored to your comfort and relaxation goals.

Natural Ayurvedic Beauty Care

Treat your complexion and hair to personalized therapies that gently cleanse, deeply moisturize, and restore a radiant, luminous glow through the power of pure nature.
