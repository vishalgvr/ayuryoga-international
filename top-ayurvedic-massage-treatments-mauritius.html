Why Choose Ayurvedic Massage for Stress Relief?

Ayurvedic massage is more than a regular body massage. It follows traditional techniques that use herbal oils, rhythmic movements and specialised treatment methods.

These treatments are traditionally valued for their relaxing and rejuvenating experiences. Some potential wellness benefits include:

  • Promoting relaxation and reducing everyday stress.
  • Helping ease muscle tension and physical fatigue.
  • Supporting a feeling of comfort and relaxation.
  • Nourishing the skin with traditional herbal oils.
  • Providing an opportunity to disconnect from daily stress.
  • Supporting a regular self-care and wellness routine.

The right treatment depends on your individual preferences, physical condition and wellness requirements.

Top 7 Ayurvedic Massage Treatments to Experience in Mauritius

Discover some popular traditional Ayurvedic treatments available at Ayuryoga International:

1. Abhyangam – Traditional Full Body Ayurvedic Massage

Abhyangam is one of the most recognised traditional Ayurvedic massage treatments. This therapy involves a full-body massage using warm medicated herbal oils and traditional massage movements. It is designed to provide a soothing and relaxing experience.

Why experience Abhyangam?
  • Promotes deep physical relaxation.
  • Helps ease everyday muscle tension.
  • Provides a nourishing herbal oil massage experience.
  • Helps you unwind after a busy day.

Abhyangam is a wonderful option for people looking for a traditional Kerala Ayurvedic massage in Mauritius.

2. Shirodhara – A Traditional Relaxation Therapy

Shirodhara is a specialised Ayurvedic therapy in which a continuous stream of warm liquid, commonly herbal oil, is gently poured over the forehead. This traditional treatment is widely appreciated for its calming and soothing experience.

Why experience Shirodhara?
  • Encourages profound relaxation.
  • Creates a calming wellness experience.
  • Provides an opportunity to unwind mentally.
  • Complements traditional Ayurvedic wellness routines.

Shirodhara is particularly popular among people looking for a peaceful and relaxing Ayurvedic experience.

3. Kizhi – Traditional Herbal Hot Pouch Massage

Kizhi is a traditional Ayurvedic treatment that uses heated herbal preparations wrapped in cloth pouches. The warm herbal pouches are gently applied to selected areas of the body using traditional techniques. Different types of Kizhi treatments use different herbal ingredients.

Why experience Kizhi?
  • Provides a comforting warmth-based treatment.
  • Helps ease everyday muscular tension.
  • Supports physical relaxation.
  • Offers a traditional herbal wellness experience.

Kizhi is an excellent treatment to explore if you enjoy herbal therapies and warm massage techniques.

4. Ksheeradhara – Traditional Medicated Liquid Therapy

Ksheeradhara is a traditional Ayurvedic treatment involving the controlled pouring of warm medicated milk or other prescribed liquids, depending on the treatment protocol. It is traditionally incorporated into specialised Ayurvedic wellness programmes.

Why experience Ksheeradhara?
  • Offers a calming treatment experience.
  • Complements traditional Ayurvedic massage.
  • Encourages relaxation and comfort.
  • Provides a distinctive traditional wellness experience.

5. Udwarthanam – Traditional Herbal Powder Massage

Udwarthanam is a traditional Ayurvedic treatment involving the application of herbal powders through specialised massage movements. Unlike conventional oil massage, this treatment typically uses herbal powders applied with upward strokes.

Why experience Udwarthanam?
  • Offers a unique herbal massage experience.
  • Helps exfoliate the skin.
  • Supports traditional Ayurvedic body-care practices.
  • Provides an invigorating alternative to conventional oil massage.

6. Kati Vasti – Traditional Lower Back Therapy

Kati Vasti is a specialised Ayurvedic treatment in which warm medicated oil is retained over the lower back within a specially prepared herbal dough boundary. This treatment is traditionally used as part of specialised Ayurvedic wellness programmes.

Why experience Kati Vasti?
  • Provides localised, continuous herbal warmth.
  • Offers a focused lower-back wellness experience.
  • Complements traditional Ayurvedic body-care practices.

7. Herbal Ayurvedic Facial – Natural Skincare Experience

Ayurvedic facials combine traditional herbal ingredients with specialised facial massage techniques. At Ayuryoga International, herbal facial treatments offer a refreshing skincare experience inspired by traditional Ayurvedic beauty practices.

Why experience an Ayurvedic facial?
  • Helps refresh and brighten the skin.
  • Provides a deeply relaxing facial experience.
  • Incorporates traditional herbal skincare ingredients.
  • Offers an enjoyable addition to your wellness routine.
